12 / Back to our four questions

What did the data
actually tell us?

01 / Did performance change?Yes—but not equally.

LD accuracy was largely stable; PM showed bigger before/after changes.

02 / Did the groups differ?They showed different patterns.

The TikTok group had the strongest average PM decline. The others were steadier or improved.

03 / Did the tasks differ?Very clearly.

Ongoing word decisions were more consistent than remembering a planned action.

04 / Did faster mean less accurate?Not consistently.

There was no clear overall speed–accuracy trade-off in participant-level averages.

13 / Final conclusion

The biggest difference was not in recognizing words.
It was in remembering an intention.

What we learned

Prospective Memory varied substantially across interruption conditions, and the TikTok group showed the clearest average decline. LD was comparatively stable.

What we cannot conclude

These exploratory patterns do not prove that TikTok causes memory loss, establish lasting effects, or replace the study's official statistical analysis.
